Workflow teardown · fitness-coach project

Unpack the journey
before the integrations.

A concise review of the planned path from first visit to session payment.

Scope-based conceptThis is a teardown of the project scope shared so far—not an audit of a live website, the coach’s current operations or deployed integrations. Each item below is a recommendation to verify.
01 / Journey map

Where should the system help?

Start with the visitor’s goal. Define rules and owners before automating.

Journey stepQuestion to resolveUseful system roleHuman checkpoint
01 / DiscoverUnderstand the coachProfile and coaching offer.
Is the offer easy to understand?Can visitors tell what training is available and what happens next?
Keep information clearPresent accurate service details and guide visitors to the right next step.
Coach owns the contentConfirm descriptions, suitability and expectations. No AI decision needed here.
02 / ExploreCompare trainingDifferent training options.
What details help a choice?Confirm which formats, goals and constraints should be shown before naming packages or prices.
Capture the visitor’s choiceStructure the selected option so booking begins with useful context.
Coach defines fitPersonal suitability and any special training needs stay reviewable.
03 / AskGet a question answeredAssistant or direct contact.
Which answers are approved?Identify common questions and a trusted source for each answer.
Retrieve and draft within scopeUse approved coach information, then ask for missing context.
Escalate uncertaintyHealth, injury, unclear or out-of-scope questions go to a person.
04 / BookSelect a sessionChoose an available time.
What confirms a reservation?Check availability, time zone, duplicate attempts and confirmation ownership.
Validate before confirmingOnly show a session as booked after the calendar or booking system confirms it.
Make exceptions visibleUnavailable slots and conflicting requests need a clear follow-up path.
05 / PayComplete paymentPayment option in scope.
Which payment states exist?Confirm how success, cancel, decline and retry appear to the coach and visitor.
Track confirmed provider stateDo not treat a pending or declined payment as paid.
Provide recoveryGive the visitor a next step and the coach a clear record when payment fails.
06 / TrustReview real outcomesClient result stories.
Is the story accurate and approved?Verify context, permission and what can be published.
Present approved evidenceKeep claims tied to real client stories and context.
Client consent mattersCoach confirms permission and any requested changes before publication.
